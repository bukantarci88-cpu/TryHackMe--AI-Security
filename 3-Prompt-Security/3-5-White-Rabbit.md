**White Rabbit** room on TryHackMe, which focuses on one of the most important emerging AI threats: **Prompt Injection**.

Unlike traditional attacks that target code, prompt injection targets the instructions given to an AI model. By crafting specific inputs, attackers may be able to bypass restrictions, expose hidden prompts, or influence the model's behavior in unintended ways.

Key takeaway: Prompt-based restrictions alone are not a security control. Organizations need layered defenses, including proper authorization, monitoring, and secure AI architecture.

As AI becomes more integrated into business applications, understanding AI-specific attack vectors such as prompt injection is becoming an essential cybersecurity skill.
